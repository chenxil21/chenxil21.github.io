Join us

Build creative tools with us.

The Creative eXperience Lab (CXL) at the University of Victoria studies how people create and builds intelligent systems for visual creation that are more controllable, more accessible, and more accountable to the creators behind the data. If you are excited about generative image models, vector & sketch representations, or human-in-the-loop creative tools, I would love to hear from you.

Generative image models

Controllable, accessible, and attribution-aware image generators that fit real creative workflows.

2D visual representations

Structure for how images are made: Vector graphics, sketches, neural fields and more.

Interactive creative tools

Human-in-the-loop systems that keep the artist in control of the result.

Who I'm looking for

  • PhD & MSc students: funded positions (including TA support), apply Fall 2026 for a Fall 2027 start. Apply to UVic CS and reach out directly. (MSc students can transfer to the PhD program if we're a good fit.)
  • Undergrad researchers: UVic students looking for a research project are welcome to get in touch.
  • Postdocs: primarily candidates with external funding, or those keen to apply for funding together.

What you'll get

  • Close, hands-on mentorship, and a real say in shaping the group together.
  • A blend of creative and technical work: graphics, ML, HCI.
  • A direct path to top-tier venues (SIGGRAPHs) through an established publication track record.
  • Help connecting with academic and industry partners for collaborations and internships.

How to apply

  1. Apply via UVic CS and mention my name, if you're applying for a PhD or MSc position.
  2. Email me with the subject line including "CXL" and the role you're interested in.
  3. Attach your CV.
  4. Write a short paragraph on what you want to work on and why CXL.
  5. Include links to any projects, code, or portfolio that show your creative or technical work.

If you have already applied to UVic Computer Science, mention it in your email so I can look out for your application.
